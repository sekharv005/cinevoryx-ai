About Cinevoryx AI

Cinevoryx AI is an intelligent movie research and discovery assistant powered by Google Gemini and the TMDB movie database.

The application uses an agentic AI workflow in which a large language model interprets user requests, selects appropriate tools, retrieves movie information, and generates natural-language responses.
